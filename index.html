What work should not exist anymore?

Repetitive work

Copying data, weekly updates, manual entry, and document handling.

Fragile work

Tribal knowledge and processes that depend on one person.

Hidden work

Searching files, inboxes, proposals, and systems for information that already exists.

Coordination work

Approval chasing, handoffs, status checks, and work about work.

Rework

Corrections, reformatting, and rebuilding the same output again.
