Cortex — agent harness

Cortex is Carapace's flagship product: a governed agent harness for real business work. Discord is where we post the latest releases, roadmap notes, and implementation patterns first.

  • Context management Control what the model sees — scope, redact, and assemble context deliberately instead of dumping everything in.
  • Intent-aware routing Route work by what it is trying to do, not just which model is cheapest or loudest.
  • Model independence Swap local, private-cloud, frontier, or specialized models without rebuilding your workflows around one vendor.
  • Custody and governance Policies, approvals, audit logs, and boundaries so teams can move faster without removing judgment.
  • Fling capture Capture useful work from anywhere — browser, files, email, and other surfaces — into governed execution paths.
  • Signal-to-work intake Turn incoming signals into ranked, reviewable tasks instead of letting them die in inboxes and chat threads.
