How work gets routable

Employees should not have to memorize which model to use. Cortex maps work to capability aliases—summarize, extract data, classify, compare, draft, and route—then selects from frontier, local, private-cloud, open-weight, and specialized models.

Why routing matters

Each request can be matched by capability, cost, speed, context, privacy, and policy. Every workflow stays inspectable, including why work went where it did and where human approval entered the loop.
