# Website Plan for Carapace

_Last updated: 2026-03-24_
_Status: checkpoint after major implementation pass_

## 1. Goal
Restructure the site so it clearly serves multiple audiences:

- individual users who want the free/open-source download
- small business buyers who want practical AI help
- commercial / enterprise evaluators who need licensing and implementation clarity

The site should no longer treat all of these as the same visitor.

---

## 2. Core positioning

The website should communicate this simple model:

**Carapace helps businesses use AI practically through consulting, implementation, licensing, and reusable solutions.**

Supporting model:

**Carapace is free and open core for personal use. Business use runs through commercial licensing, implementation, and optional support.**

## 4. What pages currently do

## Home (`index.html`)
### Current role
Front door for the overall story.

### What it now does
- leads with practical AI for everyday businesses
- points users toward consultation, platform, or free download
- frames Carapace as business help first, platform second
- uses a value carousel instead of static/expandable cards

## How We Help (`business.html`)
### Current role
Service and implementation page.

### What it now does
- explains consulting / pilot / implementation path
- describes who the service is for
- routes people to the intake form
- uses a carousel for the top business explanation section

## Licensing (`licensing.html`)
### Current role
Explains the commercial model.

### What it now does
- keeps top-level explanation static and clear
- shows personal use vs business use
- uses a carousel for what businesses usually need

## Platform (`carapace.html`)
### Current role
Explains the product/platform itself.

### What it now does
- gives a lighter platform explanation
- supports both personal exploration and business evaluation
- uses a carousel for the core platform capability section

## About (`about.html`)
### Current role
Holds mission, philosophy, and company identity.

### What it now does
- explains who Carapace is and why it exists
- carries mission statement and philosophy
- uses a carousel for the belief pillars

## Solutions (`solutions.html`)
### Current role
Translates Carapace into practical business problems.

### What it now does
- frames Carapace around real workflow categories
- adds concrete business examples
- uses a carousel for solution areas
