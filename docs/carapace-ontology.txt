# The Cortex Ontology

## Private Intelligence That Understands How Your World Actually Works

Cortex is built on a different kind of AI foundation.

Most AI tools treat your information as a pile of disconnected files, messages, tasks, and prompts. They can summarize what you give them, but they rarely understand how your work actually fits together.

Cortex is different.

Cortex organizes your information into a living map of your real-world processes: the people you work with, the tasks you repeat, the files you depend on, the decisions you make, the tools you use, the handoffs that create friction, and the outcomes you care about.

We call this the **Cortex Ontology**.

It is the layer that allows your AI to understand not just what your data says, but what your data is doing.

A customer inquiry becomes a conversation.

Cortex connects them.

It sees the patterns across your work and helps turn scattered information into useful, private, actionable intelligence.

# What Cortex Understands

Cortex does not only understand “things.”

It understands how things participate in your work.

A file is not just a file.
It may be part of a proposal, a contract, a client relationship, or a compliance process.

An email is not just a message.
It may be part of a sale, a support issue, a scheduling conflict, or a promise that needs follow-up.

An invoice is not just a record.
It is part of cash flow, customer trust, bookkeeping, and business health.

A task is not just a checkbox.

# The Six Layers of Cortex Intelligence

## 1. Your World

Cortex starts by understanding the actual things in your world:

* people,
* customers,
* vendors,
* files,
* emails,
* messages,
* invoices,
* projects,
* appointments,
* devices,
* notes,
* jobs,
* and tools.

This gives your AI a grounded map of what exists.

## 2. Your Processes

Cortex then identifies how work moves.

It learns the repeated flows that make up your business or personal system:

* onboarding,
* quoting,
* scheduling,
* installation,
* fulfillment,
* customer support,
* billing,
* follow-up,
* planning,
* documentation,
* and review.

This allows Cortex to understand work as a sequence of living transformations, not isolated tasks.

## 3. Your Decisions

Cortex helps identify what decision is actually being made.

Should this be approved?
Should this be sent?
Should this be scheduled?
Should this be escalated?
Should this be automated?
Should this stay private?
Should this wait for human review?

By understanding the decision, Cortex can provide better support than a generic AI assistant.

## 4. Your Actions

Cortex does not only answer questions. It can help move work forward.

Depending on your permissions, Cortex can help:

* draft responses,
* organize files,
* prepare quotes,
* summarize conversations,
* create tasks,
* update records,
* schedule follow-ups,
* flag risks,
* recommend next steps,
* and automate repeatable work.

But action is always governed by boundaries.

## 5. Your Boundaries

Cortex is built around consent, privacy, and control.

Every action can be limited by rules such as:

* local-only processing,
* human approval required,
* never send externally,
* draft only,
* read-only,
* client-visible,
* private,
* reversible,
* or high-trust action.

This gives you AI that can help without overstepping.

## 6. Your Feedback

Cortex improves through correction.

When you edit, approve, reject, override, or correct something, that feedback becomes part of the system.

Mistakes become learning signals.
Repeated patterns become workflows.
Friction points become improvement opportunities.
Successful outcomes become reusable process knowledge.

Cortex does not just automate your work.

It helps your work become more self-aware over time.
