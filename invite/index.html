The gap

The expensive part was the programmers.

Big companies never waited for a vendor to invent exactly how they quote a job. They paid programmers and built it. You were told that was only for huge companies. You still know how your shop works. That was always the valuable part.

Most owners cannot name the real problem when they walk in. The morning is finding it together. The afternoon is building a first version. You leave with files on your laptop, and they are yours.

What we look for

Three kinds of problems

Keep payroll and the credit card tool. Build the piece that almost fits, or the bridge between tools that do not talk.

01

Stop renting the important part

You pay monthly for a tool that almost fits. Keep payroll and credit cards. Build the quoting page, intake form, or job sheet yourself.

02

Connect tools that do not talk

The website form sits in email. Photos die on a phone. You do not have to switch platforms. You need a small bridge in between.

03

Stop being the copy-paste

Someone, often you, retypes the same job from email to spreadsheet to the paid tool. Encode the rule. Leave people for judgment.
