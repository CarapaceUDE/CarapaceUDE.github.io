Patreon Early Access Builds

Carapace open-sources its projects under the MIT License. Patreon is how we distribute official pre-compiled binaries during early access — not a paywall on the software itself.

You are not gated from building or distributing binaries. Anyone may view, modify, compile, and share builds from our source code. If you distribute a community build, remove Carapace branding (the Carapace name and marks) so it is not presented as an official release, and retain the MIT copyright notice for Carapace LLC.

Why we do this

When we open-source a new project, we are usually months into development with limited runway. Distributing official binaries through Patreon serves two practical goals at once:

  • Early access for capable users. Pre-compiled builds let developers and power users try the software immediately, file useful feedback, and help us harden releases before wider distribution — without requiring everyone to compile on day one.
  • Financial support when it matters most. Patreon is primarily a way to fund ongoing development during the fragile early phase of each open-source project. It is support, not a license to use the source.

We are working toward making official binaries available directly from Carapace for everyone. Patreon is the bridge until those releases are ready.

Your rights

Source code — everyone. Our open-source repositories are MIT-licensed. You may use, modify, build, and distribute software compiled from that source, provided you include the copyright notice for Carapace LLC and the MIT license text.

Community builds. You may share binaries you compile yourself or receive from another community member. Those builds are not official Carapace releases. Do not use Carapace branding in a way that implies endorsement or origin from Carapace LLC.

Official binaries — supporters. If you obtain an official binary from Carapace LLC through Patreon, you receive a personal, non-transferable license to install and run that binary for your own use. You may not redistribute, resell, or publicly host official binaries unless we give written permission.

Building from source

You do not need Patreon to use our software. Clone the repository, follow the README build instructions, and run your own binary. If you get stuck, join our community Discord — we offer limited setup and build help there for both supporters and anyone compiling from source.

A common pattern in open source

Keeping source fully open while offering supporter channels for convenience, early access, or funding is well established. Other projects you may already use take a similar approach:

  • Godot Engine Free and open source under the MIT License, with Patreon and other supporter channels funding core development.
  • OBS Studio Completely open source; official project funding and contributor support run partly through Patreon and sponsorships.
  • Home Assistant The platform is open source and self-hostable; Nabu Casa provides a paid supporter path that funds development without locking out builders.
  • Krita Free, GPL-licensed creative software sustained by donations and community funding while remaining fully buildable from source.

We follow the same principle: the source stays open, the community can always build, and supporter channels help us ship faster while the project is still finding its footing.
