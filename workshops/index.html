Start with the basics.
Build as you learn.

No coding experience needed. We explain each step in plain language, then help you try it yourself.

  1. Understand the setup

    Learn what an AI assistant can help with. A harness is the software around an AI model that connects it to tools and gives it instructions and limits.

  2. Set up Grok Bot

    Follow a guided setup, choose your settings, and get your assistant working.

  3. Practice on everyday tasks

    Try drafting a customer reply, organizing notes, or planning a task. Learn to give clear instructions and check the results.

You will leave with a working setup and the confidence to keep practicing.
