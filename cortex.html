Cortex

Private Control Plane

A model-independent layer that decides which model, tool, or environment handles each request based on policy, cost, speed, and context.

Your intelligence layer should belong to you.

Open core Governed

Experimental software

Cortex is still experimental. Use controlled environments, test carefully, and keep human review in the loop.

Best for

Teams evaluating a governed control plane.

  • Operators who want inspectable routing and approvals
  • Businesses testing local or private-cloud AI workflows
  • Builders exploring the open-core evaluate path

Not for

When you need a finished SaaS product on day one.

  • Teams expecting zero setup or governance work
  • Use cases that need vendor-managed multi-tenant AI only
  • Production without human review in experimental builds
