The Product Promise

Intelligence you control. Workflows you can trust.

Cortex is not another AI chat product. It is the layer between your business and the AI ecosystem: context-aware, model-independent, approval-gated, and designed around custody from the start.

Hero Features

The six capabilities that define Cortex.

These are the public-facing features that carry the main business value: less context waste, smarter routing, provider flexibility, custody-first controls, capture from anywhere, and intake that becomes real work.

01

Proprietary Context Management

Control what the model sees.

Cortex decides what context is worth spending and what should stay outside the prompt.

  • Structured context handling
  • Summaries, cached reads, and retrieval surfaces
  • Cleaner prompts with less unnecessary exposure
02

Intent-Aware Routing

Route the work by what it is trying to do.

Cortex evaluates whether a request is asking to summarize, extract, classify, compare, draft, search, route, automate, approve, or execute.

  • Capability aliases for common work types
  • Routing by cost, speed, privacy, and policy
  • Less burden on employees to choose the model
03

Model Independence

Use the right model without marrying the vendor.

Cortex can work across local models, open-weight models, private deployments, and frontier APIs.

  • Local, private-cloud, and frontier destinations
  • Better cost control as providers change
  • One control layer across changing model markets
04

Custody + Governance

Move faster without removing judgment.

Approvals, logs, transcripts, budgets, routing rules, and execution boundaries keep supervised automation visible.

  • Credential-conscious execution patterns
  • Human-in-the-loop approvals
  • The model cannot leak what it never received
05

Fling Capture

Capture useful work from anywhere.

Fling lets an operator grab context from a webpage, feed card, document, browser session, or selected content and send it to the right destination.

  • Intake, chat, job queue, webhook, feed, agent, or workflow
  • Less manual copy-paste between tabs
  • Fast movement from context to action
06

Signal-to-Work Intake

Turn incoming signals into governed tasks.

Browser captures, links, social traffic, email, documents, media, and pasted text can pass through trust checks and landing queues before becoming work.

  • Operational signal instead of disconnected noise
  • Approval gates before execution
  • Intake that routes toward useful automation

Why It Matters

The business stays at the center.

Cortex lets a company use AI without reorganizing itself around one hosted black box. Work can happen locally, remotely, or in hybrid patterns depending on security, cost, performance, and integration needs.

Carapace remains the implementation partner: scoping the workflows, deploying the environment, defining approval boundaries, tuning routing behavior, and turning useful patterns into repeatable infrastructure.

Control the destination. Not the vendor.

The company decides what can move, where it can go, who approves it, what is logged, and which model or tool is appropriate for the job.

Custody: files, credentials, sessions, and workflows can remain under client governance.
Flexibility: local, open-weight, private, frontier, and specialized systems can coexist.
Inspectability: decisions, approvals, routing, transcripts, and outcomes become reviewable.
