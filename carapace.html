Carapace

Carapace is an open-source AI operations platform for running, supervising, and shaping AI-assisted workflows with clear operator control.

Start with it as a personal download, or use it as the base for a deeper business deployment with Carapace LLC.

What it does

  • Runs AI-assisted workflows in a focused desktop environment
  • Gives operators clearer visibility into tools, context, and execution
  • Supports coordination, oversight, and intervention points across active work
  • Provides a practical base for experimentation, orchestration, and future package-driven extensions

Who it’s for

  • Individuals who want to explore Carapace for personal use
  • Builders testing AI-assisted workflows locally
  • Teams evaluating how governed AI operations could fit their environment
  • Businesses that may later want deployment and integration support

Why it matters

Carapace is built to make AI workflows more legible and more operable, not just more automated.

Instead of scattering activity across tabs, scripts, and disconnected tools, Carapace gives you a more unified place to run human-supervised AI work.

Status

Carapace is still experimental software. Use controlled environments, maintain human review, and do not treat the current build as production-safe.

Best for

  • People who want more visibility than a pile of chat tabs gives them
  • Builders experimenting with supervised agent workflows
  • Teams exploring a more controlled way to run AI-assisted work

Not for

  • Anyone looking for a finished, production-safe enterprise platform today
  • Hands-off automation with no operator review
  • Teams that do not want to think about oversight, control, or workflow design

Open source software, paid implementation

Carapace is available as open-source software for personal use and experimentation. For teams that want help deploying it in real workflows, Carapace LLC provides consultation, integration, setup, and support.

The product stays accessible. The business model is built around setup, adaptation, governance, and support.
