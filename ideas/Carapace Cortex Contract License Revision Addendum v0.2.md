# Carapace / Cortex Contract and License Package

## Revision Addendum v0.2 — Clean Replacement Package

**Prepared for internal business planning and review by qualified Florida counsel.**

This addendum is intended as a clean replacement package for the sections identified below in the Carapace & Cortex Contract and License Package, Draft 0.1. It is not intended for production use until approved and conformed by qualified legal counsel.

## 1. Cortex Community Edition License Agreement

### Replace Section 15 (Governing Law) in full with:

### 15. Governing Law; Venue

This Agreement and any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, Cortex Community Edition, or their subject matter are governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

Subject to applicable non-waivable consumer-protection law, the parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of (a) the state courts located in Miami-Dade County, Florida, or (b) if federal subject-matter jurisdiction exists, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, Miami Division. Each party waives any objection based on improper venue or forum non conveniens.

Nothing in this Section limits any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be waived under applicable consumer-protection law.

### Insert new Section 15A immediately after Section 15:

### 15A. Attorneys' Fees

In any action or proceeding between Carapace and a user arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the prevailing party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and taxable costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees incurred on appeal or in bankruptcy proceedings, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This Section does not limit any non-waivable right or remedy available under applicable consumer-protection law.

## 2. Cortex Commercial License Agreement

### Replace Section 22 (Indemnification) in full with:

### 22. Indemnification

#### 22.1 Definitions

For purposes of this Section:

“Claim” means any third-party claim, demand, action, suit, arbitration, investigation, inquiry, subpoena, civil investigative demand, or other proceeding brought or initiated by a private party, governmental authority, regulator, or other third party.

“Losses” means judgments, settlements, damages, liabilities, penalties and regulatory fines to the extent lawfully indemnifiable, and reasonable attorneys' fees and other reasonable out-of-pocket costs and expenses, including reasonable costs of investigation, forensic review, preservation, collection, processing, review, redaction, production, and response to subpoenas, civil investigative demands, or other compulsory legal process.

#### 22.2 Client Defense and Indemnity

Client will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Carapace, its affiliates, and their respective owners, officers, directors, employees, contractors, agents, successors, and assigns from and against any Claim and resulting Losses to the extent arising out of or relating to:

1. Client Data, including Client's collection, creation, acquisition, accuracy, quality, legality, disclosure, transfer, storage, security, retention, deletion, or other processing of Client Data;
2. Client's or any Authorized User's acts, omissions, instructions, configurations, workflows, automations, access controls, credential practices, or use or misuse of Cortex or any Carapace services;
3. Client's or any Authorized User's violation of applicable law, regulation, court order, or the rights of any third party;
4. Client's use, publication, distribution, implementation, or reliance on outputs, including use without appropriate human, legal, compliance, safety, or professional review;
5. Client's breach of this Agreement, an Order Form, a Statement of Work, or any incorporated policy or exhibit;
6. Client's or any Authorized User's breach of, or failure to comply with, the terms, policies, licenses, or restrictions of a third-party service, platform, model, data source, content provider, account, or integration; or
7. Client's handling of personal data, personal information, confidential information, credentials, authentication data, financial information, health information, biometric information, children's data, regulated data, or other sensitive data, including any failure to obtain required rights, notices, consents, authorizations, or approvals; any unauthorized or unlawful collection, use, disclosure, transmission, retention, or deletion; or any security incident, privacy incident, or regulatory noncompliance caused by Client, an Authorized User, or a person acting on Client's behalf.

Client has no obligation under this Section to the extent a Claim is finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction to have resulted from Carapace's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or material breach of this Agreement.

#### 22.3 Carapace IP Indemnity

Carapace will defend and indemnify Client against a third-party claim alleging that the Cortex Platform, as provided by Carapace and used by Client in accordance with this Agreement, directly infringes that third party's United States patent, copyright, or trademark.

Carapace has no obligation for a claim arising out of or relating to:

1. Client modifications;
2. Client Data;
3. third-party services, models, content, software, or materials;
4. combination of Cortex with products, services, data, or processes not provided by Carapace, where the claim would not have arisen without the combination;
5. use outside the scope of this Agreement or contrary to Documentation or Carapace's written instructions;
6. continued use after Carapace provides a non-infringing replacement, modification, or reasonable instruction to stop use; or
7. community-created archetypes, templates, prompts, scripts, integrations, or other materials not provided by Carapace.

If the Cortex Platform becomes, or in Carapace's reasonable opinion is likely to become, subject to an infringement claim, Carapace may, at its option: (a) procure the right for Client to continue using the affected portion; (b) modify or replace the affected portion with a materially equivalent non-infringing alternative; or (c) terminate the affected portion and refund any prepaid, unused fees allocable to the terminated portion. This Section states Carapace's entire liability, and Client's exclusive remedy, for intellectual-property infringement claims.

#### 22.4 Indemnification Procedure

The indemnified party must: (a) give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the Claim, except that delay will relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent materially prejudiced by the delay; (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement, subject to this Section; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

The indemnified party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense. The indemnifying party may not settle a Claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent if the settlement: (i) admits fault or wrongdoing by the indemnified party; (ii) imposes injunctive, non-monetary, or ongoing obligations on the indemnified party; or (iii) does not fully and unconditionally release the indemnified party from the Claim. Consent may not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

#### 22.5 Allocation

If a Claim is subject to indemnification only in part, the indemnifying party's obligations will be allocated in proportion to the extent the covered acts or omissions caused the Claim or Losses, as determined by agreement of the parties or a court of competent jurisdiction.

If approved by counsel, insert the following:

## 3. Counsel Confirmation Checklist

Florida counsel should confirm before release:

1. **Florida enforceability.** Confirm the governing-law, exclusive-forum, indemnity, liability-allocation, and prevailing-party provisions under the facts of Carapace's Florida nexus and anticipated client locations.
2. **Community Edition assent and consumer treatment.** Confirm whether Community Edition users may qualify as consumers; whether any forum, fee, warranty, liability, or indemnity language must be limited or separately presented; and the required click-through presentation and evidence of assent. Production implementation should use an affirmative, unchecked acceptance control, a conspicuous link to the exact version before acceptance, and retained records of version, timestamp, notice, and acceptance. Do not rely on passive browsewrap alone.
3. **Regulatory fines.** Confirm which civil penalties, administrative assessments, or regulatory fines may lawfully be indemnified and whether public-policy limits require additional exclusions. The proposed text covers them only “to the extent lawfully indemnifiable.”
4. **Subpoena and investigation costs.** Confirm the intended scope for third-party subpoenas, civil investigative demands, regulator inquiries, preservation, forensic review, e-discovery, redaction, and production costs, including whether reimbursement should apply when no formal Claim is filed.
5. **Emergency relief.** Decide whether the optional Section 25B is needed. The carve-out should remain narrow so it does not swallow the exclusive Miami-Dade venue provision.
6. **Sensitive data.** Confirm that Section 22.2(7) reaches Client-caused mishandling of personal, confidential, credential, financial, health, biometric, children's, regulated, and other sensitive data, including missing consents, unlawful processing, security incidents, privacy incidents, and regulatory noncompliance.
7. **Liability-cap interaction.** Confirm that Client's defense and indemnity obligations, confidentiality/privacy breaches, unlawful conduct, and payment obligations are excluded from any contractual liability cap if that is the intended business allocation. Without an express carve-out, Section 21 may be argued to cap indemnity obligations.
8. **Insurance and solvency.** Consider requiring appropriate cyber/privacy, technology errors-and-omissions, and commercial general liability coverage; indemnity language alone does not guarantee a collectible recovery.
9. **Defined terms.** Add or conform “Authorized User” and any privacy/security terms across the Commercial License, Order Forms, Data Processing Addendum, Security Exhibit, and Acceptable Use Policy.
10. **Other package documents.** Determine whether the same Florida venue, attorneys' fees, Client indemnity, sensitive-data, investigation-cost, and liability-cap rules should be incorporated into the Strategy & Support Retainer Terms, Discovery & Integration Sprint Agreement, Order Form, Data Processing Addendum, and Security Exhibit rather than relying only on incorporation by reference.

## 4. Merge Instruction for Counsel

Please treat this Revision Addendum v0.2 as a clean replacement package for the identified sections of the current Carapace / Cortex Contract and License Package.

The revisions are intended to: (1) require Client defense and indemnity for Client-caused claims, unlawful conduct, Client Data, Client use of outputs, third-party-term violations, and Client-caused mishandling of sensitive data; (2) establish a mutual prevailing-party attorneys' fees provision; and (3) apply Florida law with exclusive Miami-Dade venue, including the Southern District of Florida, Miami Division where federal jurisdiction exists.

Please confirm final Florida enforceability, Community Edition consumer and click-through treatment, treatment of regulatory fines and subpoena costs, the interaction between indemnity and the limitation-of-liability provision, and whether the optional emergency-injunctive-relief carve-out should be included.
