# Carapace Website Update Plan

*Derived from the Carapace/Cortex pitch deck (10 slides) vs. the current live site (5 pages: homepage, about, business, solutions, carapace platform).*

---

## Executive Summary

The pitch deck reveals a significantly more mature, specific, and strategically sharp positioning than what currently lives on the live site. The new verbage introduces **seven major themes** the current website does not adequately express:

1. **Intelligence sovereignty** — "Own Your Intelligence" (Slide 1)
2. **Model independence** — "Control the Destination, Not the Vendor" (Slides 1, 5, 7)
3. **Secrets isolation** — "Your AI Should Never See Your Secrets" (Slide 2)
4. **Human middleware elimination** — "Your Employees Are Acting as APIs" (Slide 3)
5. **Work elimination framework** — 5 categories of wasteful work (Slide 4)
6. **Consultancy over platform** — "You Don't Need an AI Department" (Slide 6)
7. **Inspectable trust** — "Trust Should Be Inspectable" (Slide 9)
8. **Structured adoption** — "Small Wins First. Scale Later." (Slide 8)
9. **Sprint CTA** — "Discovery & Integration Sprint" (Slide 10)
