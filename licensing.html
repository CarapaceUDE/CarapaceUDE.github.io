Licensing

Carapace is free for personal use. When it becomes part of a business workflow, the path shifts to commercial licensing, implementation, and optional support.

Simple version

If you are exploring Carapace for yourself, the free path is the right place to start.

If you want to use Carapace inside a business workflow, that moves into the commercial path.

Why this exists

This keeps Carapace accessible for personal use while giving the business a sustainable model for commercial deployment, implementation work, and long-term support.

Choose the path that fits

Personal use

For learning, self-hosting, and individual experimentation.

  • Free download path
  • Best for exploration and personal workflows
  • Good for getting familiar with the platform
Business use

For team workflows, commercial deployments, and company operations.

  • Commercial licensing path
  • Pairs naturally with setup and implementation help
  • Fits teams using Carapace in real business operations

What businesses usually need

Commercial licensing

The right to use Carapace in real business workflows.

  • Applies when Carapace becomes part of business operations
  • Keeps the open-core model sustainable
  • Can be discussed alongside pilots or implementation
Implementation

Help with setup, workflow fit, and integration work.

  • Consulting around a real workflow
  • Pilot deployments to prove value
  • Reusable integrations and workflow package development
Optional support

Ongoing help if your team wants it.

  • Refinement and troubleshooting
  • Workflow expansion over time
  • Not required for every engagement
Reusable packages

Repeatable solutions can become reusable business assets.

  • Integrations and workflow bundles
  • Useful patterns from consulting can be packaged
  • Supports the long-term marketplace direction
