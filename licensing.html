How the model works

One open entry point. One business path when the work becomes real.

The licensing model is designed to feel readable from the outside. Individuals can learn the platform without friction. Businesses can evaluate fit without immediate lock-in. Commercial use gets its own clear agreement once Cortex becomes part of live operations.

Why publish everything

Good licensing should not depend on a sales call to reveal the basic shape. This page puts the policy, the practical path, and the current agreements in one place so the operational decision is easier to inspect.

What stays true

Control should survive growth.

Whether you are reading the free license, continuously evaluating as a smaller business, or moving into commercial use, the throughline is the same: the boundaries should be inspectable and the deployment path should stay under your control.

What does not happen here

There is no mandatory support fee disguised as a product requirement. There is no need to pretend a pilot and a scaled deployment are the same thing. The page should make it clear when you are simply exploring, when you are still evaluating, and when you are operationalizing.
