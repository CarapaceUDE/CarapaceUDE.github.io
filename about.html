About Carapace

Carapace is the company: a private AI infrastructure consultancy and the team behind Cortex, our open-core, model-independent software platform.

We are not here to sell AI hype. We are here to help real businesses use automation in ways that save time, improve workflows, and give existing teams more leverage.

Who we are

Carapace is the company and infrastructure partner.

We build Cortex, deploy it into real business workflows, and turn what works into reusable integrations, packages, and durable business systems.

Why we exist

AI adoption stalls less from a lack of intelligence than from a fear of exposure.

Credentials, client records, private files, and strategy do not belong in an uncontrolled model context. Carapace exists to make useful AI possible without surrendering ownership or operational control.

Intelligence sovereignty

Your intelligence layer should belong to you.

Cortex sits between your business and the AI ecosystem. Carapace installs, governs, and improves it so you can use the right frontier, local, private-cloud, or specialized model without locking your strategy to one vendor.

Trust you can inspect

Trust is a feature, not a promise.

Policies, approvals, routing decisions, model choices, and audit logs are designed to be visible. You can verify the data path, see what was accessed, and understand how information moved.

Our mission

Carapace exists to put practical AI automation into the hands of everyday businesses, helping small teams and existing employees become more capable without losing the human strengths that make them valuable.

The goal is not replacement. The goal is leverage.

How the model works

  • Cortex is free for personal use
  • Business use runs through commercial licensing
  • Implementation and integration are available through consulting
  • Support is available when it is useful, not mandatory by default

Our approach

Discover → Deploy

We identify repetitive, fragile, hidden, coordination, and rework problems, then launch the strongest candidate in a private local, cloud, or hybrid environment.

Measure → Expand

We measure hours saved, costs reduced, adoption, and operational impact. Only then do we expand into more workflows and departments.

You do not need an AI department

You need the benefits of one.

We become your private AI infrastructure team: helping select hardware, deploy locally or in the cloud, manage integrations, define policies and approvals, maintain auditability, and continuously improve the system as the model landscape changes.
